🍥 ANGELA'S CINNAMON ROLL RECIPE 🍥

Welcome to the kitchen! 👨‍🍳 Get ready to make some warm, soft, sweet, and delicious cinnamon rolls!

🥣 Ingredients

  • 🍞 For the Dough

    • ¾ cup warm milk
    • 2¼ teaspoons yeast
    • ¼ cup granulated sugar
    • 1 egg plus 1 egg yolk
    • ¼ cup butter
    • 3 cups bread flour

  • 🍂 For the Filling

    • ⅔ cup dark brown sugar
    • 1½ tablespoons ground cinnamon
    • ¼ cup butter

👨‍🍳 Let's Start Baking!

  1. Mix the warm milk with the yeast, sugar, eggs, and egg yolk.
  2. Melt the butter and add it to the mixture.
  3. Add the flour and mix until it becomes a dough.
  4. Knead the dough for 10 minutes. 💪
  5. Transfer the dough into a large bowl and cover it with plastic wrap.
  6. Let the dough rise for 2 hours. 😴
  7. After the dough doubles in size, roll it into a large rectangle.
  8. Make the cinnamon filling.
    • Melt the butter.
    • Add the brown sugar.
    • Add the cinnamon.
    • Mix them together. ✨
  9. Spread the cinnamon filling onto the dough.
  10. Roll the dough into a Swiss roll. 🍥
  11. Cut the roll into 3 cm sections.
  12. Place the sections flat into a baking tray.
  13. Preheat the oven to 350°F or 180°C. 🔥
  14. Bake the rolls for 20–25 minutes until lightly brown.

🎉 Time to Enjoy!

Your cinnamon rolls are finally ready! 🍥

Grab a plate, share them with your family or friends, and enjoy your delicious homemade cinnamon rolls! 😋
